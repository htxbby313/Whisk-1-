# Whisk Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Effective Date:** November 17, 2026  
**Entity:** Whisk, Inc.  
**Contact:** privacy@whisk.travel  

This Refund & Cancellation Policy applies to all paid **"Passport"** tier subscriptions and single-trip unlocks purchased through **Whisk** and processed by our third-party payment processor.

## 1. Free Previews vs. Paid Passport Tier
- Whisk allows travelers to create a profile and generate an initial **free itinerary preview** prior to purchasing a Passport plan.
- Upgrading to a Passport subscription (single-trip unlock, monthly plan, or annual plan) provides immediate digital access to full multi-day schedules, interactive routing, booking links, and unlocked features.

## 2. Subscription Cancellation
- **Cancel Anytime:** You may cancel recurring monthly or annual Passport subscriptions at any time before your next renewal date through your account billing portal or by emailing **privacy@whisk.travel**.
- **Effect of Cancellation:** Upon cancellation, your Passport benefits remain active until the end of your current paid billing cycle, after which your account automatically reverts to the free Wanderer tier without further charges.

## 3. Refund Eligibility & Cooling-Off Windows
- **Duplicate Charges or Technical Failure:** You are entitled to a 100% refund if you were billed twice in error or if a verified Whisk platform outage prevented access to your unlocked Passport itinerary within 48 hours of purchase.
- **Unused Subscriptions (7-Day Window):** First-time Monthly or Annual Passport subscribers who have **not** unlocked additional full itineraries or booked through Passport links may request a full refund within **7 calendar days** of initial charge by emailing **privacy@whisk.travel**.
- **Single-Trip Digital Unlocks:** Once a single-trip unlock has been applied to reveal a full itinerary, the digital service is deemed consumed and is non-refundable except in cases of technical defect or mandatory statutory rights.
- **EU/UK 14-Day Right of Withdrawal:** If you reside in the EEA or UK, you generally have a 14-day statutory right to withdraw from online purchases; however, by clicking to immediately unlock digital Passport itineraries before the 14-day period expires, you expressly consent to immediate performance and acknowledge waiver of the withdrawal right upon full digital delivery, unless defective.

## 4. Third-Party Travel Bookings
Whisk facilitates discovery and links to third-party airlines, hotels, and tour providers. **We do not process or hold funds for third-party travel bookings.** Refunds, cancellations, or date changes for flights, lodging, or activities are governed exclusively by the respective provider's policies.

## 5. Receipts & Billing Support
Every successful charge triggers a **payment receipt notice** (and booking verification notice where applicable). To request a refund or dispute a charge, contact **privacy@whisk.travel** with your account email and receipt ID within 30 days.

## 6. Governing Law & Consumer Rights
This Policy is governed by **California** law, without prejudice to mandatory local consumer-protection or statutory refund laws in your country or state of residence.
