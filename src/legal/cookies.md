This Cookie Policy explains how **Whisk** uses cookies, local storage, and similar tracking technologies when you visit or use our web and mobile application hosted on Firebase Hosting.

## 1. Categories of Cookies & Storage Used

| Category | Provider / Technology | Purpose | Duration | Opt-Out Available? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Strictly Necessary** | Firebase Authentication, Firebase Hosting, Session tokens | Keeps you signed in (Email, Username, Google OAuth, or Apple Sign-In), enforces security rules, and remembers essential UI state | Session to 30 days | No (required for the app to function) |
| **Preferences / Functional** | Browser `localStorage` / Firestore sync | Saves your travel-style preferences, cookie choices, and active itinerary draft preview | Persistent until cleared | Yes (via browser settings / account reset) |
| **Analytics & Performance** | Google Analytics (optional) | Measures traffic, feature popularity, error rates, and trip-planner completion metrics | Up to 14 months | Yes (Cookie Banner / GA Opt-Out) |
| **Marketing / Sharing (If Enabled)** | Attribution tags (if deployed) | Measures campaign performance and prevents duplicate referral attribution | Up to 90 days | Yes ("Do Not Sell or Share" / GPC) |

## 2. How to Manage or Opt Out of Cookies
- **Consent Controls (EEA/UK & Applicable Regions):** Non-essential analytics and marketing cookies are disabled until you consent. You may withdraw or update consent at any time via **Cookie & Privacy Choices** in the footer.
- **CCPA / Global Privacy Control (GPC):** Whisk honors valid **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** signals sent by supported browsers as an automatic opt-out of any cross-context behavioral sharing or data sale.
- **Browser & Google Analytics Controls:** You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings or install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Blocking strictly necessary authentication storage may prevent sign-in and Passport access.

## 3. Demo Session Isolation
Any internal demonstration session uses short-lived session storage isolated from live production customer tokens and is cleared immediately upon signing out.
