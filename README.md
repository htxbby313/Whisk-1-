## 🧭 About Whisk

**Whisk** ([whisk.travel](https://whisk.travel)) is a next-generation travel concierge and itinerary planning web application designed for modern travelers who value time over tourist lines. Powered by **Google Gemini** and **Firebase**, Whisk moves beyond generic, exhausting top-10 checklists by engineering human-paced, immersive schedules calibrated to your personal travel stamina.

Every day-by-day route is grounded in real-time **Google Search and Google Maps** data—providing verified operating hours, honest neighborhood recommendations, dynamic weather backups, and seamless partner bookings for boutique hotels, flights, and curated experiences.

---

## ⚡ Core Sections & Signature Features

### 1. The Algorithmic Energy Curve Scheduler
Say goodbye to vacation burnout. Whisk calibrates each travel day with an intelligent stamina curve—scheduling high-energy exploration during morning peaks, relaxed cafe strolls and siestas during midday lulls, and romantic aperitivos and rooftop dining at twilight.

### 2. Dynamic Rainy-Day Weather Swaps
Travel plans shouldn't fall apart when it rains. Whisk continuously monitors destination forecasts and automatically generates indoor cultural pivots—swapping rainy outdoor ruins or cliffside trails for covered arcades, private galleries, historic bookstores, and cozy wine bars.
